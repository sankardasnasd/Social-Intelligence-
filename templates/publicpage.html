What happens after you upload

Five automatic steps run in the background, so you get results in minutes with no manual review work.

  1. 1

    Identify

    Detects whether the upload is a video, audio clip or image.

  2. 2

    Read

    Speech becomes text, on-screen text is extracted and frames are analysed.

  3. 3

    Check safety

    Vulgar, inappropriate and harmful content is flagged for review.

  4. 4

    Predict

    Topic, sentiment, trend, reach and best posting time are estimated.

  5. 5

    Recommend

    Safe reels reach viewers by interest, region and watch history.
